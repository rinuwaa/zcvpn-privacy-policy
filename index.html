ZC VPN Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

ZC VPN ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the ZC VPN application.

1. Information We Collect

ZC VPN is designed to provide VPN connectivity and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or passwords.

Technical information may be processed when necessary to establish and maintain a VPN connection. This may include connection status, selected server information, device or application information, and network information required for the VPN service to function.

2. VPN Connections

When you connect to a VPN server through ZC VPN, your network traffic may pass through the selected VPN server. Information necessary to establish, maintain, and secure the VPN connection may therefore be processed by the VPN infrastructure.

3. Logs

ZC VPN does not intentionally maintain a permanent record of your browsing activity or the websites you visit through the VPN connection.

Temporary technical information may be processed when necessary for connection management, troubleshooting, security, or service operation.

4. Third-Party Services

ZC VPN may use third-party infrastructure or services to provide VPN connectivity, networking, hosting, security, or application functionality. Such services may process technical information required to provide their respective services.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through the application and its supporting infrastructure. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

ZC VPN does not intentionally collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the service, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or ZC VPN, please contact the ZC VPN developer or service administrator through the official support channel associated with the application.

9. Acceptance

By using ZC VPN, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
